About me

I’m a UI/UX and Graphic Designer who is also learning web development, so what I design is shaped by how it will actually be built.

I believe good design starts with a clear question: who is this for, and what should they be able to do? I work from that question outward, testing ideas in Figma and refining until the interface feels obvious to use.

    How I work

    1. Understand the brief

      We agree on the goal, the audience, and what success looks like before any pixels move.

    2. Research and explore

      I look at references and competitors, then sketch several directions instead of one.

    3. Design and refine

      The strongest direction becomes a full design, improved through your feedback.

    4. Deliver and improve

      You receive organised files, and I stay available for revisions after handoff.
